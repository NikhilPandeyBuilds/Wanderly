About Rajasthan

Rajasthan is a living open-air museum of majestic forts, kaleidoscopic bazaars, and opulent maharajah palaces. Spanning from the pink-hued avenues of Jaipur to the blue maze of Jodhpur, the golden ramparts of Jaisalmer, and the romantic lake reflections of Udaipur, it promises an unforgettable royal odyssey.

Must-Visit Places

Suggested 6-Day Itinerary

Click through each day to explore the curated timeline.

Day 1-2: Jaipur Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal & Bazaars

Climb the grand bastions of Amer Fort in morning light. Visit the honeycomb facade of Hawa Mahal and Jantar Mantar observatory. In the evening, explore Johari Bazaar for blue pottery, textiles, and authentic ghewar sweets.

Highlight: Sunset from Nahargarh Fort overlooking Jaipur

Day 3: Jodhpur Mehrangarh Ramparts & Blue City Lanes

Explore the towering cliffside fortress of Mehrangarh with its armory and royal palanquins. Stroll the winding indigo alleyways of the old quarter and sip saffron lassi near the Clock Tower.

Highlight: Ziplining over Mehrangarh Fort Moats

Day 4: Jaisalmer Living Fort & Thar Desert Glamping

Wander through the golden sandstone living fort of Jaisalmer with its carved Jain temples. In late afternoon, head into the Thar desert for a camel safari across Sam Sand Dunes, followed by stargazing, folk music, and Kalbelia dance.

Highlight: Desert Campfire, Stargazing & Folk Dance

Day 5-6: Udaipur Lake Pichola Boat Cruise & Palaces

Conclude in the City of Lakes. Tour the sprawling City Palace complex overlooking Lake Pichola. Enjoy a sunset royal boat cruise past Jag Mandir and Taj Lake Palace.

Highlight: Lake Pichola Sunset Cruise
