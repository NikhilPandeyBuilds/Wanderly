About Goa

Goa is an unforgettable tapestry of Portuguese colonial heritage, palm-fringed coastlines, lively night markets, and sleepy inland backwaters. From the vibrant shacks of the north to the peaceful secluded coves of south Goa, it is the ultimate haven for both relaxation and discovery.

🏖️ Golden Beaches
⛪ Latin Quarter
🦐 Coastal Cuisine
🌅 Sunset Cruises

Must-Visit Places

  • Fort Aguada & Sinquerim Lighthouse
  • Fontainhas (Latin Quarter in Panaji)
  • Dudhsagar Cascading Waterfalls
  • Palolem & Agonda Crescent Beaches
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus (UNESCO Site)
  • Anjuna Flea Market & Chapora Fort

Suggested 4-Day Itinerary

Click through each day to explore the curated timeline.

Day 1: Arrival, Beachside Leisure & Sunset Shacks

Check into your coastal boutique resort. Spend an easy afternoon on Candolim or Ashvem beach. Watch the sun dip into the Arabian Sea while sampling fresh catch and coconut kokum cooler.

Highlight: Golden Hour at Thalassa or Curlies

Day 2: Panaji Latin Quarters & Old Goa Cathedrals

Stroll past pastel yellow and indigo Portuguese villas in Fontainhas. Visit the grand 16th-century churches of Old Goa, followed by a traditional Goan fish curry thali in Panaji.

Highlight: Walking Architecture Tour in Fontainhas

Day 3: Dudhsagar Waterfalls & Organic Spice Plantation

Take a morning jeep safari into the Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary to witness the roaring four-tiered Dudhsagar falls. End the afternoon enjoying a traditional banana-leaf buffet at Sahakari Spice Farm.

Highlight: Jungle Safari & Vanilla-Cardamom Walk

Day 4: Palolem Kayaking & Departure

Head to peaceful Palolem Beach for morning sea kayaking to Butterfly Beach. Pick up cashew feni and spiced handicrafts before heading to Mopa or Dabolim airport.

Highlight: Dolphin Spotting in Quiet Bays
