About Kashmir

Known affectionately as heaven on earth, the Kashmir Valley captivates with serene mirror-calm waters of Dal Lake, ornate Mughal terraced gardens, fragrant saffron fields of Pampore, and the world-renowned alpine meadows of Gulmarg and Pahalgam.

Must-Visit Places

  • Dal Lake & Nigeen Lake Houseboats
  • Gulmarg Apharwat Peak (Phase 1 & 2)
  • Betaab Valley & Aru Valley in Pahalgam
  • Shalimar Bagh & Nishat Mughal Gardens
  • Pampore Saffron Fields
  • Sonamarg (Meadow of Gold & Thajiwas Glacier)

Suggested 5-Day Itinerary

Click through each day to explore the curated timeline.

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar & Dal Lake Shikara Sunset

Check into your hand-carved cedarwood houseboat on Dal Lake. Enjoy hot saffron Kahwa tea on the deck followed by a 2-hour shikara cruise gliding through the floating vegetable markets and lotus gardens at dusk.

Highlight: Sunset Shikara across Char Chinar

Day 2: Gulmarg High Altitude Gondola & Pine Meadows

Drive up to Gulmarg, traversing pine-scented mountain bends. Ride the famous Gulmarg Gondola to Apharwat Peak at 13,780 feet for panoramic snow views. In winters, enjoy skiing and snowshoeing.

Highlight: Apharwat Summit & Strawberry Valley

Day 3: Pahalgam, Betaab Valley & Lidder River

Travel along the gushing Lidder River to Pahalgam. Wander through lush Betaab Valley, named after the Bollywood classic, and ride ponies up to Baisaran Meadow (Mini Switzerland).

Highlight: Riverside Trout Dining & Pine Treks

Day 4: Sonamarg & Thajiwas Glacier Exploration

Venture to Sonamarg, gateway to Ladakh. Hike or take a pony ride to the foot of the Thajiwas Glacier. Stop by walnut groves and return to Srinagar for an authentic Wazwan dinner.

Highlight: Glacier Walk & Traditional Wazwan Feast
