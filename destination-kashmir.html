About Kashmir

Kashmir is a journey in layers: a still lake at dawn, a warm cup of kahwa, a meadow opening between pine trees and a road that keeps revealing another mountain.

Travel tips

Pack layers, carry comfortable shoes and leave room in the itinerary for weather. Respect local customs and ask before photographing people.
