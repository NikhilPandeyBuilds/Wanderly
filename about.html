The idea behind the project

About Wanderly

Travel is more than reaching a destination. It's about the places you discover, the people you meet and the stories you bring back.

A student-made explorer

Made for curious travellers.

Wanderly is a student-designed travel exploration platform created as an HTML and CSS college project. It brings together destination inspiration, simple trip ideas and a clear visual system that is easy to understand and enjoyable to browse.

Everything you see here is sample content for a practical project. The destinations, packages, testimonials and artwork are designed to demonstrate structure, styling, responsive layouts and accessible HTML.
